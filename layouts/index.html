01

Research

My work sits at the interface of statistical methodology and biomedical science. These are the four questions currently organizing that program.

R1

Bayesian inference with incomplete covariates

Semiparametric survival models and evidence measures for settings where clinically meaningful predictors are censored or only partially observed.

Survival analysis · Bayesian computation · objective Bayes

R2

Geometry of whole-brain dynamics

Manifold and connectivity methods for identifying multiple functional regimes in high-dimensional fMRI time series.

fMRI · network neuroscience · covariance geometry

R3

Nonstationarity in spatial biology

Statistical models that treat spatially varying covariance—not only changing means—as a source of biological structure in transcriptomic data.

Spatial statistics · omics · covariance modeling

R4

Scientific machine learning with structural priors

Shape-aware learning for tumor grading and protein-model ensembles for computational immunotherapeutic design.

Shape analysis · transformers · protein modeling
