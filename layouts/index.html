Puneet Velidi

Statistics & neuroimaging
University of Victoria

I am an MSc student in Statistics at the University of Victoria, working with Farouk Nathoo and Michelle Miranda. My work primarily focuses on statistical neuroimaging, especially fMRI.

I also founded ChimeraML, a deeptech research studio backed by NVIDIA Inception, Labs4, and Mitacs. Previously, I studied Statistics and Economics at Cornell University.

Research interests

My research interests are in spatial and functional data analysis, primarily applied to statistical neuroimaging, especially fMRI. I also work on applications to gene expression data.
