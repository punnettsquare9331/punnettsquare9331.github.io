About

Methods with rigor.
Tools with taste.

I’m a thesis-based MSc student in Statistics at the University of Victoria, co-supervised by Farouk Nathoo and Michelle Miranda.

My work moves between statistical methodology and scientific application: censored covariates, spatial transcriptomics, neural manifolds, shape analysis, and computational immunotherapy.

Before graduate school, I worked across research at Harvard Medical School / MGH and Weill Cornell Medicine, and engineering at Walmart Global Tech.
